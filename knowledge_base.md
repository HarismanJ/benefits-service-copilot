#BENEFITS SERVICE COPILOT — DEMO KNOWLEDGE BASE

##Purpose
This document contains synthetic employee-benefits guidance for demonstration
purposes. It is not financial, legal, insurance, tax, or eligibility advice.
A qualified benefits advisor must review recommendations before they are sent.

##RENEWAL REVIEW

When a client reports a significant renewal increase, collect:
- Current and renewal premiums
- Renewal effective date
- Percentage increase
- Current benefit schedule
- Employee and dependent enrollment
- Recent claims or utilization information, if available
- Employer and employee contribution structure
- Plan changes made during the previous year
- Client budget and objectives

Do not assume that changing providers will reduce costs. Plan design, employee
demographics, participation, claims experience, coverage levels, and market
conditions may all affect pricing.

##PLAN COMPARISON

Plans should be compared using consistent information. Important comparison
areas include:
- Life insurance
- Accidental death and dismemberment coverage
- Short-term and long-term disability
- Extended health coverage
- Prescription drug coverage
- Dental coverage
- Vision coverage
- Paramedical services
- Travel coverage
- Employee assistance programs
- Eligibility requirements
- Waiting periods
- Coverage maximums
- Deductibles and reimbursement percentages
- Exclusions and limitations
- Employer and employee premium contributions

A lower premium does not necessarily mean a better plan. Differences in
coverage, exclusions, maximums, service, and employee needs must be considered.

##NEW PLAN INTAKE

For an organization without an existing plan, collect:
- Number of eligible employees
- Province or provinces of operation
- Employee occupations
- Full-time and part-time employee counts
- Desired benefits
- Desired effective date
- Estimated budget
- Employer contribution percentage
- Whether dependent coverage is required
- Whether employees currently have individual coverage

##HUMAN REVIEW

The copilot may organize information, identify missing details, search approved
documents, and prepare draft communications. A human advisor must review plan
recommendations, pricing comparisons, coverage interpretations, and final client
communications.

# Fictional Benefits Plan Catalogue

## Important Notice

The plans below are fictional and exist only for demonstrating the Benefits Service Copilot. They are not real insurance products, quotes, contracts, or financial advice.

The copilot may identify potential matches, but a licensed benefits advisor must confirm eligibility, coverage, exclusions, pricing, and suitability.

## Plan 1: Essential Shield

- Plan ID: ES-100
- Availability: Ontario
- Eligible group size: 5–50 employees
- Cost tier: Low
- Prescription drugs: 70% reimbursement
- Basic dental: 70% reimbursement
- Major dental: Not included
- Vision: Not included
- Paramedical services: $300 per person annually
- Life insurance: $25,000 per employee
- Employee assistance program: Optional
- Best suited for: Small employers prioritizing lower costs and basic coverage
- Main trade-offs: Lower reimbursement and limited dental and vision coverage

## Plan 2: Balanced Choice

- Plan ID: BC-200
- Availability: Ontario
- Eligible group size: 10–100 employees
- Cost tier: Medium
- Prescription drugs: 80% reimbursement
- Basic dental: 80% reimbursement
- Major dental: 50% reimbursement
- Vision: $200 every 24 months
- Paramedical services: $500 per person annually
- Life insurance: One times annual salary
- Employee assistance program: Included
- Best suited for: Employers wanting balanced coverage and predictable costs
- Main trade-offs: No orthodontic coverage and moderate annual maximums

## Plan 3: Enhanced Plus

- Plan ID: EP-300
- Availability: Ontario
- Eligible group size: 25–250 employees
- Cost tier: High
- Prescription drugs: 90% reimbursement
- Basic dental: 90% reimbursement
- Major dental: 60% reimbursement
- Orthodontics: 50% reimbursement up to a lifetime maximum
- Vision: $300 every 24 months
- Paramedical services: $750 per person annually
- Life insurance: Two times annual salary
- Employee assistance program: Included
- Best suited for: Employers prioritizing employee attraction and comprehensive coverage
- Main trade-offs: Higher expected premiums and potentially higher renewal exposure

## Plan 4: FlexCare Modular

- Plan ID: FM-400
- Availability: Ontario
- Eligible group size: 15–150 employees
- Cost tier: Medium
- Prescription drugs: 80% reimbursement
- Basic dental: 80% reimbursement
- Major dental: Optional
- Vision: Available through a flexible health spending account
- Paramedical services: $400 per person annually
- Health spending account: $500 per employee annually
- Life insurance: One times annual salary
- Employee assistance program: Included
- Best suited for: Employers wanting flexibility for employees with different needs
- Main trade-offs: Employees must manage spending-account limits, and optional benefits may increase costs

## Plan-Matching Rules

When identifying potential plan matches:

1. Only use plans contained in this fictional catalogue.
2. Check the employer’s province and employee count first.
3. Consider requested benefits, cost tier, and concerns with the current plan.
4. Present no more than two strongest potential matches.
5. Explain why each plan may fit and identify its trade-offs.
6. Never claim that a plan is guaranteed to be eligible or less expensive.
7. Never invent pricing, coverage, providers, or plan details.
8. State what information is still needed before a recommendation can be finalized.
9. Require review by a qualified benefits advisor.
